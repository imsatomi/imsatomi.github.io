<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">
MotionTales is an interactive digital textbook for physical education in elementary school. Children's
movements are captured and turned into a story on screen, inviting them to improvise and express
themselves with their whole body. The work received an Honorable Mention Award (top 5%) at the
SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 Educator's Forum.
</div>
<div data-lang="ja" markdown="1">
MotionTalesは、小学校の体育のためのインタラクティブなデジタル教科書です。子どもたちの動きを取り込んで画面上の物語に変えることで、身体全体を使った即興的な表現を引き出します。SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 Educator's ForumでHonorable Mention Award（上位5%）を受賞しました。
</div>
